## Integration gates

1. **Vercel:** Verify the deployment points to the intended source and exact HTTPS `APP_ORIGIN`; verify server-only `DATABASE_URL`. `PAYMENTS_ENABLED=false` and `JUDGE_PAYMENTS_ENABLED=false` must remain set. `JUDGE_TREASURY_CONTRACT_ID` and `JUDGE_TREASURY_WASM_SHA256` are optional public-value overrides; absent overrides use the verified account/hash in source. Incorrect overrides fail the server's deployed-WASM hash check. No Vercel variable was inspected or changed in this phase.
2. **Neon:** Review [DEPLOYMENT.md](DEPLOYMENT.md), identify the production database, app role and migration role, establish a recoverable backup, and check existing objects and privileges read-only. The user reports development migration/concurrency checks passed. Production status and permissions remain unverified. Never rerun the initial empty-database migration. Apply the separate judge lock migration only after explicit authorization; then verify its privileges and perform the scoped live concurrency test against an authorized target. Do not clear pending intents or drop tables as rollback.
3. **TLS:** Keep certificate and hostname verification enabled. Review `sslmode=verify-full` and the provider's trusted CA requirements with the installed node-postgres/libpq versions before changing any secret or production configuration.
4. **Wallet and ABI:** A judge must authenticate with NEP-413 and explicitly sign each Testnet transaction in a compatible wallet. The owner signs create, authorize, execute and refund; no developer signer is involved. The browser prepares 100 Tgas calls matching the contract ABI. Supported provider transaction signing, implicit accounts, account switching and redirects have not been verified in a real browser. Check recipient existence and ownership independently; syntactic validity is not identity verification. A 0.01 NEAR escrow also requires a nonrefundable 0.03 NEAR storage contribution plus gas.
5. **Recovery:** Claims are owner scoped and serialize outstanding operations in PostgreSQL. The server verifies signer, receiver, method, arguments, deposit, finalized contract state, and receipts before marking settlement complete. UNKNOWN or hashless outcomes remain locked; recover the original hash from the wallet/explorer and reconcile. No blind retries. A callback that never completes can leave a request pending; no admin override exists. Keep the contract ID/hash and database settings intact during incident recovery.
6. **Controlled pilot:** Only after separate approval of the production migration, web application rollout/configuration and live transactions: test one wallet's full lifecycle, receipts and refresh/reconnect; test a second independent wallet's isolation; then repeat with the developer laptop and local services off. Never claim success based on a submitted hash alone.

Public constants and gates do not deploy a contract or activate payment signing. Preserve the historical `agentledger-treasury.testnet` and its imported evidence.
